Privacy Policy

Ride Atlas is built with strict privacy standards. We believe in providing open, high-quality motorcycle routes without personal surveillance.

Location & Navigation

Your location privacy on the road is strictly protected:

  • Browser-Local Geolocation: If you allow location access to center the map, your coordinates are processed purely within your browser session. Your coordinates are never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.
  • Turn-by-Turn Navigation: External navigation links open Google Maps in a new tab. We do not transmit your ride history or navigation telemetry.

Optional Analytics

Ride Atlas optionally uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) solely to measure aggregate catalog usage and prioritize route curation. Analytics is strictly guarded by four sequential gates:

  1. Only active in official production builds.
  2. Only active on our production domain ({{ production_url }}).
  3. Only active when enabled by configuration with a valid property ID.
  4. Only active when you explicitly grant consent.

If any single condition is not met, no Google tracking scripts are fetched and zero network requests are made to Google.

What Is Tracked (With Consent)

When consent is granted, only five explicit catalog interaction events are recorded:

  • catalog_object_open: View of a route, road, or destination (kind and ID only).
  • catalog_filter_apply: Route category filter selection.
  • catalog_search_empty: Recorded when a search query returns 0 results (count only; your search terms are never recorded).
  • catalog_maps_click: Clicks on external navigation links (object ID only; destination URLs are never recorded).
  • catalog_gpx_download: Clicks to download route GPX files.

What is never collected: Names, email addresses, user passwords, search queries, IP addresses (anonymized/dropped), or GPS location coordinates.
