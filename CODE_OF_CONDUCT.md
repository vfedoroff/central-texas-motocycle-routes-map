# Ride Atlas Code of Conduct

Ride Atlas welcomes contributors and riders of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone deserves respectful participation, regardless of identity, disability, nationality, religion, appearance, or riding experience.

## Community standards

- Be considerate and help newcomers.
- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people. Give constructive feedback and accept corrections.
- Respect privacy. Ask permission before sharing someone else's personal information, location, or photos.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, make unwanted sexual advances, or use degrading language or imagery.
- Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern or helps resolve it.

## Contributing to a riding community

People ride different motorcycles and have different abilities and comfort levels. Avoid shaming someone for their bike, equipment, pace, or decision not to ride.

Keep route discussions factual. Distinguish personal experience from verified information, and treat reports of road closures, access restrictions, and unsafe conditions with care. Do not pressure others to ride beyond their abilities, trespass, or take risks to contribute route information.

Route accuracy, sourcing, and licensing requirements are covered in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Scope

This policy covers project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces, plus public activities where someone officially represents Ride Atlas.

## Reporting concerns

Report Code of Conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer at [code@v79tx.com](mailto:code@v79tx.com).

Do not post sensitive incident details in public issues. A report should describe what happened and include relevant links or screenshots when available.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and share information only as needed to investigate and respond. A maintainer involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation. Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include a correction, written warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban. Serious incidents may warrant an immediate ban. Maintainers should explain decisions to the people involved when appropriate.

## Attribution

The general community standards and enforcement approach draw on [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The riding guidance is specific to Ride Atlas.
